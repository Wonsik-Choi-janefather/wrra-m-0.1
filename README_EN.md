# WRRA-M 0.1 — English Overview

## Research decision

WRRA-M is opened as a separate research branch and does not modify WRRA Core 1.0. Its first task is to test a correspondence:

- the seven internal dimensions used in four-dimensional M-theory compactification;
- seven predimensional degrees in WRRA;
- a dimension filter that renders a finite 3+1-dimensional phenotype and particle channels.

## Architecture

- **Source and Law:** invariant substrate and transition law.
- **Predimensional state \(P_7\):** seven independent degrees, with no assumed spatial interpretation.
- **Residue:** the present record of earlier transitions; its ordering is the candidate origin of physical time.
- **Dimension filter:** the transformation from the predimensional state to the finite phenotype.
- **Gravity:** the phenotype inheritance of the total upstream kinetic and stress structure, not a twelfth coordinate.
- **Dark matter candidate:** twist stress—gravitational output from upstream structure that has no displayed matter phenotype.

## Exact result and boundary

Two oriented \(G_2\) seven-sectors plus one invariant channel produce fifteen channels. Under restriction to the color subgroup \(SU(3)\), the branching multiplicities exactly match the color content of one Standard Model generation:

\[
\mathbf{1}\oplus\mathbf{7}_A\oplus\mathbf{7}_B
\downarrow_{SU(3)}
=2\cdot\mathbf{3}\oplus2\cdot\bar{\mathbf{3}}\oplus3\cdot\mathbf{1}.
\]

The result is exact only in the color sector. The next decisive tasks are to derive weak pairing, one hypercharge operator, chirality, three generations, masses, a Lorentzian 3+1 metric, and a transferable numerical twist-stress law.
