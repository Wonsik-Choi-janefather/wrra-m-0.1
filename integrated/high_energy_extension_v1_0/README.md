## Principal corrections made during review

1. Planck time is a comparison scale, not the WRRA shutter period.
2. Event discreteness and physical-time discreteness are not identified.
3. The micro/macro boundary is a family of record-stability conditions, not one universal number.
4. Active component occurrences are not the same as the number of primitive component types.
5. Four primitive types remain a candidate only under additional grammar assumptions; they are not asserted.
6. Neutrino “harmony” means a retained multi-mode phase representation; it is not yet an upstream derivation of PMNS mixing.
7. Components, generations and harmonics allocate an existing normalized energy budget; they do not multiply cosmic energy.
8. No new stage 0.13 is introduced. The development series closes at 0.12.
