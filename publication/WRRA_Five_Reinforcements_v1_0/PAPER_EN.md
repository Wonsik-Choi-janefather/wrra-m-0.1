## Abstract

We consolidate five finite, executable reinforcements of Wonsik Reality-Renderer Architecture (WRRA): discrete-to-continuum scaling, conditional SOURCE/carrier dynamics, an information-load/gravity connection, physical readout of zeta-derived phases, and freedom accounting with joint falsification constraints. Verified constants and calibrated observations are legitimate construction inputs; explanatory reproduction and post-calibration conditional predictions are assessed through the actual WRRA transformations. Conservative coarse-graining preserves sector ownership and supports second-order finite receiver convergence. A positive generator executes a registered finite resource swap and a declared spatially modulated exchange. A two-state admission law reproduces the inherited empirical radial acceleration relation (RAR), while quadratic twist normalization connects its exposure to a local storage/traction contract. An adopted QUMOND-type receiver supplies a nonspherical three-dimensional diagnostic. Seventy inherited zeta phases are carried through a conservative multiport instrument into calibrated energy and detection outputs; a prescribed positive Hamiltonian executes the same endpoint. A 30-entry scoped ledger distinguishes calibration, derived coefficients, state-space dimension and active fitting freedom. It reveals a rank-one local-gravity response to four released coordinates and joint zeta readout constraints that survive unknown common efficiency and coherence. All seven main/verifier execution pairs and 19 cross-module checks pass in the recheck. These coordinated modules do not constitute a newly executed complete GR/Standard Model/CMB universe. Autonomous/local control, reset thermodynamics, metric-pressure completion and species-specific coupling remain open.

It preserves calibrated relationships, credits inherited physics, and distinguishes newly adopted contracts from results following from them. It reports internal computational verification, not an external referee decision or new observational validation.
