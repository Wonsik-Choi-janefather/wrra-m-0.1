Upstream 1D numbers, intermediate 2D numbers, and final 3D phenotype

Core readout: prime → 1D; otherwise even → 2D; otherwise → 3D.

Abstract

This note defines a finite WRRA construction assigning prime, even-composite and odd-composite addresses to upstream, intermediate and final phenotype layers. A prime is assigned an interval, an even composite an orientation-preserving quotient band, and an odd composite an orientation-reversing quotient band. Their minimum Euclidean ambient embedding dimensions are 1, 2 and 3. These assignments are explicit modeling rules, rather than a conventional dimension assigned to integers.

Execution layer | Address class | Minimum ambient dimension

Intermediate | Even composite | 2

All active addresses through N=1,015,000 are classified and the inherited weights and eight-frame admission calculation are replayed. With the published calibration parameters unchanged, the address ledger reproduces 5% phenotype, 26.8% resident nonphenotype and 68.2% return. Structural eligibility and actual phenotype admission remain distinct.

2. The universe dimension-readout rule


2.1 An ordered three-stage readout

In this model, the universe reads the dimension of an active integer address n≥2 in this exact order:

Is it prime? → dimension 1

Otherwise, is it even? → dimension 2

Otherwise → dimension 3

Primality takes precedence: 2 belongs to dimension 1. The second branch is exactly the even composites; the final branch is exactly the odd composites. Address 1 is the inherited inactive vacuum reference outside this active readout. This is declared as a WRRA dimension-interpretation rule.

2.2 One topological realization of minimum dimension

One geometric realization of the readout assigns an interval X(p)=[0,p] to a prime p. For composite n, choose its smallest prime factor a and b=n/a, and form a positive-width rectangle B(n)=[0,b]×[-a/2,a/2].

sigma(n)=(-1)^n,     X(n)=B(n)/[(0,y) ~ (b,sigma(n)y)]

Even composites give an orientation-preserving annulus; odd composites give an orientation-reversing Mobius band. An interval embeds in R¹ and an annulus in R². The Mobius band is nonorientable, cannot embed in the plane, and admits an embedding in R³ [2,3]. Thus this realization has minimum ambient dimensions matching readout values 1/2/3.

2.3 Construction rules and mathematical meaning

Minimum ambient dimension preserves connectivity, orientation transport and address labels under continuous embedding. Isometry is not required. Assigning bands and parity-dependent seams is a chosen realization rule, not a theorem derived from arithmetic alone. A Mobius band is intrinsically two-dimensional; 3 here is its ambient embedding dimension.

The auxiliary bands do not establish the boundary or volume of the universe or of matter. Even integers can have odd prime factors. Dimension readout, the doubling operator, and the physical correspondence to space and energy retain their distinct roles.

4.2 Calibration provenance and interpretation

The inherited alpha and h are unchanged in this replay. A separate two-input inverse check recovers their values to floating-point precision. They were originally calibrated to composition; this note verifies that explanatory reproduction. ESA's 2013 summary reports 4.9% ordinary matter and 26.8% dark matter; the inherited WRRA targets are the rounded 5/26.8/68.2 [4].

Interpreting address weights as SI mass-energy density fractions requires the corresponding energy conversion, conservation and pressure ledger. This note reports address fractions. It performs neither a new observational likelihood analysis nor a particle-mass calculation.

5. Falsification conditions and conclusion


5.1 Reproduction and revision conditions

Revise the numerical claim if the published program fails to classify the active domain exhaustively and exclusively, violates positivity or completeness, or fails to reproduce the fractions from the specified inputs. 5.2 Validation scope

The replay covers all 1,014,999 active addresses. It includes a two-parameter inverse check and frozen-input sensitivity at N=15,000, 507,500 and 2,030,000. Incidence-based orientation, boundary-component and Euler checks are run for one annulus and three odd quotient bands. The general minimum-embedding argument is topological; representative mesh checks do not establish all physical claims.

5.3 Conclusion

The finite construction explicitly organizes primes as upstream 1D numbers, even composites as intermediate 2D numbers, and odd composites as final-layer 3D numbers. Dimension is computed as the minimum ambient embedding dimension of the assigned structure. The inherited weighting and executed admission preserve the 5%/26.8%/68.2% address ledger. The contribution is an explicit connection among arithmetic classes, topological implementation and physical readout within a common execution genealogy.
