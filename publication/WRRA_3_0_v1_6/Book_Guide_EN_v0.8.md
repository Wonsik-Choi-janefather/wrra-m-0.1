# Book v0.8 model development

This edition develops a finite WRRA toy model to explore explanatory connections among quantum gravity, vacuum energy and the dark sectors. It does not claim completed solutions. The guiding WRRA hypothesis and the implemented inputs, transformations, outputs and failure conditions are distinguished. Chapters 84–91 are current; the earlier v0.5 body is historical. Paper v1.6 and book v0.8 are being independently reviewed. Previous v1.5/v0.7 verdicts do not apply automatically to this edition.
