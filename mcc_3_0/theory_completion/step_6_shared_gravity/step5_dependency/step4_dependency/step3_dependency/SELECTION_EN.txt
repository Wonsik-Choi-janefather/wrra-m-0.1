WRRA 3.0 theory improvement Step3 v0.2: cost and minimal-state selection

Verified inputs: the same finite SOURCE, four inherited modes, coefficients,
eight-frame admission boundary, address effects, frame ledger and load interfaces.
WRRA transformation: separated cost comparison -> chunked readout -> rotation
states -> Hankel rank -> lower bound and realization of linear state dimension.
Outputs: unchanged readout/load, a locally selected rotation kernel and a minimal
eight-real-state realization of the fixed phase sequence. Twenty checks pass
in total: eight prior comparison checks, seven streaming checks and five rank checks.
Falsification: storage-dependent output, changed frame/load contributions,
omitted costs, exaggerated universal minimality or conflated state/frame counts.

For aggregate-only explanation a calibrated scalar admission is simpler. For
retaining the existing address/phase/frame explanation, preserve the phase
structure and select a chunked rotation kernel. These are different explanatory
scopes. Neither the four modes nor eight-frame boundary is uniquely selected
by the reproduced aggregate shares. Calibration and reproduction retain their
explanatory value in both scopes.

Direct evaluation uses13,692,544 trig calls. Rotation uses3,423,144,
including shared rotations, but adds47,923,904 multiplications and23,961,952
additions/subtractions. Thus total operations or physical cost are not reduced75%.
The two implementations use identical calibration and model specification.
Six kernel/chunk conditions preserve every address's admission, all eight frame
contributions and sector loads. For chunk65,536, conservative phase-work-array
bounds are10MiB direct and24MiB rotation; the full eight-frame drive table alone
is about26.1MiB. Shared inputs, SOURCE construction and process RSS are excluded.
Direct streaming trades lower work storage for more trig calls.
Three local alternating measurements after warmup give medians .211361459s
and .117584372s respectively, about44.4% shorter for rotation here. These are
kernel-only timings, not universal or whole-pipeline benchmarks. Keep direct
streaming as the reference and memory-oriented alternative.

A phase sequence with four cosine modes is a sum of eight complex exponential
poles exp(+/-i gamma_j xi). Here all poles are distinct and coefficients nonzero.
The8x8 Hankel matrix H_rs=u_(r+s) factors as V diag(a) V^T with an invertible
Vandermonde matrix and nonzero amplitudes, giving rank8. Any d-dimensional
real time-invariant linear realization has H=O R and rank(H)<=d. Therefore d>=8.
Four real two-component rotation blocks realize exactly d=8 and attain this bound.
Numerical probes at addresses9,15,105,1001 verify the factorization and rank.
Sixteen synthetic continuation samples diagnose the declared formula; they are
not sixteen observed frames. The adopted admission boundary remains eight.
Eight real state components are neither eight frames nor CCS-15 channels.

The minimum is within linear realizations of the fixed spectral rule. It does
not derive the physical necessity of four modes, the eight-frame boundary,
a unique universe structure or a minimum over nonlinear encodings. Constitutive
inputs remain explicit and legitimate. Full theory completion is not asserted.
