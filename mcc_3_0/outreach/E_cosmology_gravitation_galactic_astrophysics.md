## 6. What would falsify this cosmology branch

For a cosmologist or gravitation researcher, the useful question is not whether the upstream arithmetic language is aesthetically attractive. The relevant question is whether one frozen state survives independent physical tests.

The present branch fails or must be revised if any of the following occurs:

1. The finite-source replacement requires a new downstream SI-energy fit.
2. Galaxy rotation and lensing require independently adjusted local-gravity scales.
3. The same energy ledger fails to produce a consistent pressure and expansion history.
4. The model requires double counting of internal component ledgers to recover the macro budget.
5. A frozen response fails across galaxy samples when applied without galaxy-by-galaxy retuning.
6. A relativistic completion produces unacceptable gravitational slip or lensing behavior.
7. CMB, BAO, growth, or matter-power-spectrum calculations fail when the same background state is used.
8. Cluster or colliding-cluster dynamics cannot be described by the same stress owner.
9. Solar-System constraints exclude the required low-acceleration response.
10. The committed numerical results cannot be independently reproduced.

The currently reported lensing result is explicitly conditional and finite-patch. It is not a substitute for a full relativistic lensing calculation.
