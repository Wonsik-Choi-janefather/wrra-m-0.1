Validated inputs
The finite address/slot construction, existing arithmetic response weights, SI energy coefficients and volume exponents are unchanged. Computed outputs

Omitting the required momentum transfer violates the Einstein energy and spatial-trace constraints. Including it reduces the energy-constraint residual to about 1e-8 in common vacuum-source units. Numerical convergence and linear-amplitude scaling were also checked.

Falsification and scope
The energy/pressure/information boundary, occupation-to-density derivative, Einstein constraints and numerical convergence are explicit tests. The physical spatial preparation, interaction recipient and standard GR are declared completion assumptions. Physical address-to-3D placement, primordial amplitude/spectrum, an underlying interaction action and micro-derived anisotropic stress remain open.
The new interacting mode has not been inserted into a full radiation/recombination CAMB calculation. The earlier CMB spectra retain standard perturbation inputs. Official Planck likelihood and BAO covariance tests have not been executed; BBN abundances use the standard PRIMAT interpolation table.

This is a limited computational progress report and a concrete conservation-complete candidate, rather than a claim of full microscopic cosmological closure.
