The research began with the question of whether gravity could be understood as the weight of information. Both authors approved the final manuscript and consented to submission.

Verified inputs → WRRA transformation → outputs → failure criteria

- Inputs: finite addresses 2..1,000,000, factorization weights, specified drive families, and calibrated targets 5% and 26.8%.
- Transformation: two-stage filtering, parity-selective readout, recorded outcome–address states, common aggregation and declared energy readout.
- Outputs: fixed-exponent fraction 4.876893353%; common-denominator calibration 5% / 26.8% / 68.2%; admissible ceiling about 5.7773%; conditional q0 = -0.523. Address-dependent contrasts distinguish drive preparations even at equal aggregate fractions.
- Failure criteria: positivity/completeness violations, inadmissible targets, inconsistent shared-channel identity, or incompatible energy/pressure/expansion accounting.
