## Contribution

Fixed inputs are the inherited WRRA admission response and address weights, finite preparations, prescribed load coupling, temperature and baseline stiffness. The WRRA address–carrier transformation generates a load-dependent partition function and its covariance correction to field stiffness. Explicit states preserve both marginals and three weighted carrier operators yet have different susceptibilities. Joint and clamped equilibration require different retained information. A single scalar effective gravitational coupling is possible only under an exact proportionality condition.

Failures of the null identities, covariance Hessian, positive-stiffness criterion or source-preserving reduction would invalidate the corresponding construction. A physical realization must fix its preparation and coupling before comparing measured responses. The lattice realization discussed in v0.3 is future work.
