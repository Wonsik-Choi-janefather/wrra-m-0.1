2. Assessment of the four requested changes

A. Joint versus clamped comparison table: substantively resolved.

Table 1 correctly separates a single normalized tilt of the joint distribution from separate normalized tilts at fixed address weights. The global covariance and weighted conditional covariance entries agree with Equations (2), (3) and (10). The additional covariance of conditional address means belongs to the joint reference response. The caption's x=0 restriction appropriately prevents the table from implying an ordering of arbitrary unmatched operating points. The listed conditional moments and conditional load-class masses are sufficient for the clamped problem; the table does not wrongly claim that these are minimal encodings.

A minor notation defect was identified during review: the original caption used literal mu and sum letter strings. The authoring agent corrected this to the intended Greek mu and summation operator and added C_n=Cov_{r_n}(b_n). I verified both the current manuscript source and rebuilt PDF page 4. The correction is complete; no table-related issue remains.

B. Common-carrier and information-load explanation: resolved at the scope of this paper.

The new Section 2 paragraph explains the common carrier as a shared state substrate, the conditional carrier states as occupations in the commuting finite subfamily, and the load as a prescribed observable weighted by the address response. Together with Equation (1) and the explicit b=f_D(n)a_k witness, this gives the reader a usable state-to-load map without requiring reconstruction of the whole parent model. It also avoids equating load with Shannon entropy or raw address count. The general vector load b_a remains a constitutive choice, as it should; this paragraph does not establish a universal law tying information to gravitational charge, and the manuscript expressly disclaims such a result.

C. Concrete finite-lattice future application: resolved as a proposal, not as a completed application.

The one-particle, finite-site and internal-mode example is compatible with the classical commuting construction. Hopping plus internal equilibration can realize the joint contract under the declared canonical equilibrium assumptions. Suppressed hopping with internally relaxing prepared site subensembles can realize the clamped contract. The distinction is operationally intelligible. The text correctly limits the proposal to one particle and says that interactions and occupancy constraints require a larger configuration-state model.

The next paragraph follows the requested scientific evaluation chain: characterize stiffness, temperature and energy shifts; fix the load map and constraint; compute the covariance correction and modal response; compare measured perturbation responses and loss of positive stiffness with the fixed calculation. These are legitimate conditional tests. A laboratory realization would still need an explicit site-to-WRRA-address assignment, transition/preparation implementation, and a field degree of freedom with the stipulated restoring functional. Those are future construction tasks, not evidence already supplied here. An externally imposed field alone measures a population susceptibility; a measured inverse field response additionally needs the stated restoring and feedback mechanism. The reference to independently characterized baseline stiffness keeps the proposal compatible with that requirement. No extra experiment is necessary merely to make this explicitly theoretical proposal publishable at its declared scope.

D. Physical interpretation of C, K and M: resolved.

The new paragraph correctly distinguishes fluctuation covariance C, relaxed restoring stiffness K, and the dimensionless normalized stiffness reduction M. Its modal statements apply in K_0-normalized coordinates, so the gain (1-lambda_i)^(-1) is correctly interpreted relative to the baseline. The intuitive redistribution description is consistent with the negative Hessian of the matter free energy. The qualification at lambda_i=1 is valuable: a vanishing quadratic mode makes the linear inverse singular, but higher-order terms and the nonlinear stationary structure remain relevant. The text does not confuse this local boundary with a demonstrated global thermodynamic phase transition, causal instability, or measured change of a universal Newton constant.

3. Scientific audit under the stated evaluation standard

Verified or calibrated inputs. The finite probabilities, null identities, normalization, load alphabet, numerical stiffnesses and declared thermodynamic scales are sufficiently specified to reproduce the calculations. The particular inherited address function and exponent are used as fixed inputs, not secretly fitted to each output. The baseline gravitational scale and spatial bridge are calibration/constitutive inputs. Accepting such inputs is legitimate for this conditional model. Their use is not an independent derivation of their empirical values, and the manuscript distinguishes the two.

WRRA transformation. The specific structural contribution is the passage from the inherited weighted carrier snapshot to a new equilibrium response contract, and the demonstration that the snapshot loses information needed by that contract. The null-vector witness preserves both marginals and all inherited sector operators while changing f_D-squared fluctuation terms. Its nonzero gap follows analytically from the Vandermonde argument. The clamped gap is correctly smaller by 2z_0=0.2 in the displayed example. The separate equal-global-distribution example correctly gives clamped variances 1 and 0.64 while preserving the entire joint partition function. These are meaningful structure-dependent distinctions, even though the general statistical-mechanical identities are standard and no numerical anomaly in nature is claimed.

Outputs. The covariance Hessian, positive-definiteness criterion, inverse-kernel ordering, scalar proportionality criterion, and source-preserving Schur elimination are mathematically sound within the stated domain. Proposition 3's exact partition-family sufficiency and r-1 linear-coordinate lower bound are properly restricted to a known distinct load alphabet, positive normalized class masses, and an open field domain. The manuscript avoids extending that minimality claim to the clamped factorization or every nonlinear representation. The pressure account uses a single specified energy/free-energy construction and its derivative constraints. The numerical negative control appropriately exposes the error from dropping the hidden source correction.

Falsification and limitations. Failure of the finite identities would invalidate the implementation or its claimed algebraic realization. A physical test is distinct: preparation, thermal constraint, energy shifts, geometric basis and scales must be fixed before measured responses are compared. A discrepancy under that protocol would reject the realization within its equilibrium and small-perturbation scope. Refitting each output would change the tested model. This is a defensible statement of testability. The absence of an independently novel measured prediction is not itself a defect in a clearly labeled model-development or calibrated reproduction study. It does, however, leave empirical adequacy unestablished.

The manuscript still does not derive universal energy-gravity coupling, equivalence of the prescribed charge with all inertial or thermodynamic energy, relativistic dynamics, a stress tensor including the bath, or an observed cosmological scale. Those limitations are explicit. The proposed lattice experiment would test the finite response mechanism; without further physical identification, it would not by itself validate a theory of gravitational universality. I found no new claim in v0.3 that erases these boundaries.
