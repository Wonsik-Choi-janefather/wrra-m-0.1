# Energy and Pressure Compatibility in Finite WRRA Models
## Cold Allocations and Compensating Sectors

Wonsik Choi and Jeongin Choi — version 0.2, 9 October 2026.

Zenodo DOI (reserved for this deposit): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23251719

This fifth WRRA preprint develops cold-support conditions, a finite correlated compensating reservoir, reserve and record bounds, a convexity obstruction, and a composition with decay momentum cohorts. It is a conditional effective construction, not a claim of new fundamental matter or a stable autonomous cosmology.
