Status: This supplement adds a case registry and computation specifications to
the frozen Shutter-SOURCE Three Targets v0.2. All original files are preserved

EXISTING MACROSCOPIC OUTPUTS
Validation inputs: inherited 128-state SOURCE, static preparation, declared
relative-path coupling g=1 and 1064 nm wavelength.
WRRA transformation: exact finite-state average of relative optical phases.
Outputs: visibility 0.3586975046 at the first minimum, 21.780958380 m;
0.6081539566 at partial recovery, 37.278764320 m; distance ratio 1.7115300286.
Refutation: disagreement with the fixed preparation, exposure and visibility
curve refutes that branch. Exposure distance is not automatically device size.
Conclusion: an existing conditional macroscopic optical calculation.

Validation inputs for existing galactic rotation: simplified McMillan baryons,
SPARC-calibrated response and fixed a_t=1.1918e-10 m/s^2.
WRRA transformation: baryonic field to twist response to circular speed.
Outputs from the 27 September 2026 v1.0 manuscript, not rerun here: 226.3 km/s
at 8.2 kpc, 201.6 km/s at 25 kpc, RMS 2.42 km/s against the linearized Eilers
benchmark over 8-25 kpc. This is not an individual-data likelihood result.
Refutation: failure of the universal response or of the fixed cross-observable
closure requires revision. Conclusion: inherited astronomical-scale outputs.

M1 - VERTICAL DISK KINEMATICS
Validation inputs: fixed rotation-calibrated state, baryonic distribution,
tracer density, selection function and explicitly declared boundary conditions.
WRRA transformation: direction [n], rho_t and anisotropy q_t to Phi(R,z), then
K_z=-dPhi/dz and vertical Jeans evolution including the velocity-tilt term.
Outputs sought: K_z(R,z), sigma_z(R,z), thickness and flaring. Pending.
The v1.0 manuscript explicitly leaves the q_t closure unspecified. A rotation
curve alone does not uniquely determine the off-plane gravitational field.
Refutation: failure of the declared fixed 3D state to explain rotation and
vertical data, or the need for independent height-by-height tuning.
Conclusion: registered candidate; establish the spatial-stress closure before
computing. Calibration is allowed and its use of validation data is recorded.

M2 - LENSING FROM THE SAME GALACTIC STATE
Validation inputs: the fixed 3D stress state, source/lens distances and geometry.
WRRA transformation: derive or declare the metric potentials Phi and Psi from
the same state; alpha_hat=(1/c^2) integral grad_perp(Phi+Psi) dl in the weak-field
thin-lens approximation, followed by lens geometry. If Phi=Psi is adopted,
state that GR closure explicitly; anisotropic stress need not imply equality.
Outputs sought: deflection, convergence, shear and a conditional Einstein angle.
Pending. A spherical equivalent mass inferred from rotation is not by itself
a spatial stress distribution or an optical metric. No lensing number from a
different prior model is relabeled as this galaxy's prediction.
Refutation: failure of the fixed stress/metric state across kinematics and
lensing, or the need for independent lensing amplitudes.
Conclusion: registered candidate. Integration is credited after actual
state-to-deflection computation, rather than citation of an external formula.

M3 - COSMIC STRUCTURE GROWTH
Validation inputs: fixed background, composition, address weights, shutter,
SOURCE preparation, initial perturbations and declared tracer treatment.
WRRA transformation: SOURCE perturbations to conserved sector stress/exchange,
metric perturbations and matter transfer; D(k,a) gives f=d ln D/d ln a;
P_m(k,z) gives sigma_8(z) with an 8 h^-1 Mpc window and f sigma_8(z).
Outputs sought: P_m(k,z), scale-dependent growth and f sigma_8(z). Pending.
Seven cached CMB wavenumber transfers and their computed higher-order moments
are not a substitute for the matter spectrum on a sufficient wavenumber grid.
Refutation: conservation/constraint failure or disagreement of the fixed
growth branch with specified observations; redshift-by-redshift retuning is
recorded as failure of that fixed version.
Conclusion: registered candidate; run the required matter-transfer calculation.

COMMON ASSESSMENT
Order: validation inputs -> WRRA transformation -> outputs -> refutation ->
conclusion. Verified constants may calibrate the model. Reproduction of known
values counts as empirical consistency and explanatory performance. Outputs
computed after freezing a calibrated model are attributed to that WRRA model;
matching another theory's number does not remove attribution. The inherited
176 controls do not validate the three newly registered physical predictions.

M1: Validate the stationary/axisymmetric approximation, particularly for a warped
or disequilibrium disk. If it fails, include time evolution. Declare covariance
and acceptance criteria before the observational validation.

angles and specify angular-diameter-distance conventions and D_ls/D_s. A no-slip
Phi=Psi closure is an explicit assumption, not an automatic derived result.

survey wavenumber window and tracer model to define the effective estimator,
or compare the calculated P_m(k,z) directly.
