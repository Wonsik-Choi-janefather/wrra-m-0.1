WRRA TRANSFORMATION: FIXED CONSTITUTIVE RULES
Set g=1, a unit-coefficient model choice rather than a uniquely derived optical
constant. Retain one SOURCE phase label throughout each photon propagation.
Initial control state is I/128; average independently prepared realizations.
No per-shutter resampling, reset or label advancement is allowed in this branch.
Path A has the additional phase coupling, path B is the uncoupled reference.
delta_phi_per_shutter=(2*pi/lambda)*ell*f_D(J).
The independent and cyclic policies remain comparison controls in audit_v0_1/;
they cannot be substituted as alternative fits within this fixed version.
The selection minimizes additional control resources among the tested policies.
Count initial preparation, recording and preparation across repeated realizations;
no free reset is inferred. Resource preference is not a proof of nature's choice.

EXECUTED HAMILTONIAN AND SWITCHING LEDGER
H_int=-H_photon tensor P_A tensor F_D, with F_D=diag(f_D(J)).
Compute exp(-i H_int t/hbar), t=L/c, and verify the finite phase-gate result.
The interaction commutes with free photon energy and the retained phase label.
SOURCE-energy readout diagonal in J is consequently unchanged.
For the initially balanced paths, W_on(J)=-E_photon*f_D(J)/2;
W_off(J)=+E_photon*f_D(J)/2. Zero ensemble mean does not remove conditional work.
At 1064 nm, conditional switching-work RMS per edge=1.130202539e-27 J.
All 128 labels are recorded in interaction_and_switching_ledger.csv.
This executes the adopted constitutive connection. It is not a derivation of
universal Maxwell/SOURCE coupling, locality or Lorentz completion.

ASSESSMENT AND SCOPE
Constitutive rules are fixed and the Hamiltonian, ledger and predictions are
executed. The previous 17 controls and 11 additional controls pass. Observational
confirmation remains distinct from model construction.
N*ell=L cancels ell from the leading static visibility law. This branch therefore
does not independently measure a fundamental spatial shutter interval.
Visibility conditioned on one retained phase label is 1, as is common-mode
visibility for equally coupled arms. Loss/revival is reversible nonselective
phase averaging, not fundamental collapse or a universal macroscopic-size border.
