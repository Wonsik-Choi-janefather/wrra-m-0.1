V0.2 REVIEW AND CORRECTIONS
The main v0.1 scientific observables are unchanged. The preserved v0.1 reports
under review/ are historical evidence, superseded by this report.
1. A finite Gaussian-moment harmonic comparator uses 14 independent ternary
variables with nodes 0,+sqrt(3),-sqrt(3), weights 2/3,1/6,1/6. It matches Gaussian
moments through fourth order and reproduces two-point power. Its 3^14=4,782,969
states do not constitute a full Gaussian distribution or a unique physical law.
At ell=2..256, common-axis comparator excess kurtosis is 40.7442439509 (T),
48.5937322940 (E), 8.2394888353 (phi); the original SOURCE values are
32.2551732492, 22.8054297802, 9.7416539350. An isotropic Gaussian angular field
instead has zero connected higher cumulants. Common-axis mixing itself therefore
contributes to non-Gaussianity; the signal is not unique evidence of quantization.

The transfer is a fixed-duration phase screen at nominal L/c; no independent

The large local linear-gravity RMS is outside a controlled small-noise regime.
4. Temporal-domain checks and the already-below-target threshold edge case are
