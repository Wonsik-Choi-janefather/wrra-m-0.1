# WRRA bidirectional phenotype renderer v1.4

Wonsik Choi and Jeongin Choi. Korean and English companion papers.

This revision specifies singly even decomposition/recombination, conditional alternating shutter timing, and two distinct information interfaces: topmost–middle through WRRA quasi-prime working states; middle–phenotype through singly even states. The latter is not a proof that the former generator is implemented. The 2.5 label names a transition role, not physical fractional dimension.

The Big Bang renderer is a submodel within existing expansion, cooling and thermal history; it preserves MCC 3.0 opening, energy transfer and radiation-domination ordering. Parity sector labels and prime-power folding labels remain separate. The baseline No Extra Asymmetry Register requires the initial sign to follow existing preparation/LAW; independently chosen signs form an explicitly different branch. Black-hole interface contact remains a structural hypothesis with energy/information ownership retained.

Executed checks: 500 singly even parents, 2,000 placements; existing 630 Lorentz kinematic cases. These are arithmetic and local kinematic checks, not an executed microscopic field theory or empirical validation of the full interface proposal.

Reproduce with `python3 verify_singly_even.py` and `python3 verify_lorentz_sequential.py`. See JSON results and both manuscript PDFs. Molecular chirality is outside this paper.

Zenodo: bilingual PDF release 1.4 (publication receipt added after publication). Data and code remain on GitHub; no data archive is posted on Zenodo.
